Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 21 June 2026

Maska Agencies Limited ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy in accordance with the Constitution of Kenya 2010 (Article 31) and the Data Protection Act, 2019.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal data when you visit our website (www.maskaagenciesltd.com or related subpages), use our services, contact us, or interact with our platform in any way.

By using our website, you consent to the practices described in this Policy. We encourage you to read it carefully.

2. Information We Collect

Personal Data we may collect includes:

  • Contact information: name, email address, phone number, WhatsApp number.
  • Property-related information: preferred locations, budget, property type.
  • Identification details: ID number, where required for tenancy or transactions.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type, device information, cookies.
  • Communication records: messages sent via chatbot, contact forms, or email.
  • Location data: approximate, based on IP or provided address.

Special Category Data: We may collect limited sensitive data (e.g., health information for accessibility needs) only with your explicit consent.

3. How We Use Your Personal Data

We process your data lawfully under the Data Protection Act for the following purposes:

  • Providing property management, rental, sales, and advisory services.
  • Communicating with you about listings, viewings, and transactions.
  • Improving our website and services.
  • Marketing (only with your consent).
  • Complying with legal and regulatory obligations.
  • Preventing fraud and ensuring site security.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

We rely on:

  • Your consent.
  • Performance of a contract.
  • Legitimate interests (e.g., business operations).
  • Legal obligations.

5. Sharing of Personal Data

We may share your data with:

  • Trusted service providers (maintenance contractors, lawyers, accountants).
  • Government authorities when required by law.
  • Potential buyers/tenants (with appropriate safeguards).

We do not sell your personal data.

Cross-border transfers: Any transfer outside Kenya will comply with the Data Protection Act requirements (adequacy decisions, binding corporate rules, or explicit consent).

6. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including encryption, access controls, and regular security reviews.
